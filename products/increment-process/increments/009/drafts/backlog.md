# The backlog, and the clarify norm

The argument behind this increment's proposed set.

## Why per-product

A backlog item is a foundation-to-be of one product's future increments — a requirement or
decision with a product already implied by its content. A repo-wide pool would tag each item
with its product and route on the tag; the per-product file is that routing done by location,
with no extra machinery. A genuinely cross-product idea has no fold to land in and is not yet
an item.

## Why adoption deletes

The backlog is a working surface, not an archive. The adopting increment's sources are the
durable record of what the item became — keeping a copy behind would create a second,
staler authority for the same claim. The pull-request history holds the trail.

## Why the projection shows it

The projection is the owner's one review surface (r-2gcehlp8); what is held for the future is
part of a product's state the owner reviews. The open-questions listing set the precedent for
showing draft-adjacent material there.

## The clarify norm

The Plan tier makes the decisions its evidence already determines. Implementations stay free
to accumulate decisions as they build — autonomy depends on that regime, and it stands —
but each accumulation is a reading at the companion's ratify: a discovery, an answered
deferral, or a plan gap to feed the next increment. Where exactly the dial sits between
over-planning and implementer freedom is a subjective tuning the owner has not fixed; for now
it errs toward over-planning, with a revisit condition on the decision that says so. The
facts-surface decision in this increment demonstrates the deferral shape: the subcommand and
its output are decided, the flag layout is deferred, and the deferral is itself a ruled entry.
