# The ruling session does not show a claim's scope

The session-ruling TUI presents requirements and decisions without their `scope:` field, so a
reviewer cannot see which component a claim addresses while ruling on it. With grinbox's draft
now carrying five components and ~55 scoped foundations, ruling means holding the scoping in
your head or cross-reading the sources. Show the scope beside each entry — and grouping or
filtering the ruling queue by scope would let a sitting work one subsystem at a time.
