# a digest carries grinbox's own errors and warnings

Raised by the owner reviewing 013: a user does not see triage-time failures unless they open
the application, which is not where they live. The digest already reaches them where they are.

Carrying what grinbox itself needs to say — an operator that keeps failing, a limit being hit,
an account needing a password, a category that could not be applied — into the digest turns the
one thing the user reliably reads into the place they learn something needs them. r-x3jb6wlq
requires the interface to say it; this is about the mail saying it too.

What to work out: which classes of thing qualify, whether they are their own section or a
footer, what happens when the failing thing is the digest's own account, and how it interacts
with r-i55lgsq8 — a digest says what triage recorded, and these are grinbox's own events
rather than a message's.
