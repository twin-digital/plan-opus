# typed decisions — kinds with named slots

Generalize the typing the process has been doing piecemeal (deferrals, `pinned`, do/verify):
most decisions are instances of a small kind-set — naming, placement, format, behavior,
boundary, constraint — and each kind can be named slots plus a one-line gloss, so review
becomes slot-checking and agents cannot editorialize inside a slot.

- Derive the kind-set empirically by classifying the in-force decisions (~250) — do not
  invent it a priori; the classification is the Clarify research for this increment
- Freeform stays as the escape hatch, defaulted against
- Format-kind decisions may carry a canonical `example:` the validator checks against the
  bound schema — falsifiable, near-zero words
- The projection renders kinds as tables

Depends on the statement/notes split and the terms glossary (the wip-002 stack) landing
first, so slots compose with budgeted clauses and glossary terms.
