# decisions record considered-and-discarded alternatives

A structured slot for the alternatives a decision considered and discarded, each one line
with a one-line reason. Two purposes: an exhaustiveness forcing function at proposal time,
and an aid to future increment design sessions (the argument survives outside the frozen
drafts/ folder).

To settle when adopted:
- normative status — presumably non-normative like notes, and excluded from the fold the
  same way; visible at ratify and in design sessions
- overlap with the rejected status: a rejection closed by a replacement is already a
  recorded discarded alternative — one mechanism should not shadow the other
- budget per alternative, and a cap on the list
- the backfire risk: a forcing function invites manufactured strawmen padded in to look
  exhaustive; the authoring bar needs a test for that
