# product split along a component boundary

Mechanism and semantics for extracting a component subtree into its own product, once
components exist (see the components draft):

- moving the subtree's scoped foundations across products — needs a cross-product
  retirement/supersession pointer so citations of the moved entries resolve
- extracting the requirements both sides need into a preset each adopts
- contracts need no move: schemas and surfaces are already repo-wide pools
- what happens to implementation records and coverage filed against the old product

Also the signals that a split is due — contention, not size: ratify sittings repeatedly
spanning unrelated areas, landing conflicts between drafts that share nothing,
implementations repeatedly aborting on unrelated design landings.
